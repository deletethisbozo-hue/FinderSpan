# FinderSpan

FinderSpan is an independent Finder-inspired UI fork of SPAN Finder. It preserves the upstream file-manager engine while replacing the visual shell with a macOS-inspired WinUI treatment.

Upstream: LumiBearStudio/SpanFinder
License: GPL-3.0

This fork is not affiliated with Apple Inc. and does not bundle Apple fonts, Finder artwork, or proprietary Apple assets.
